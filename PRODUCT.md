# Transit Light-Curve Lab Product Context

## Users

Transit Light-Curve Lab serves bachelor and master STEM learners, educators, and expert explorers. Learners need clear causal relationships, readable plots, guided vocabulary, and immediate feedback. Educators need reproducible scenarios, projection-friendly layouts, visible lesson progress, and dependable exports. Experts need dense parameter access, deterministic calculation modes, diagnostics, timing history, and explicit validation.

## Product Purpose

Transit Light-Curve Lab is a browser-only scientific workspace for exploring exoplanet transits, exomoons, binary eclipses, photometry, and timing diagnostics. Simulation and Guided Labs are equal workflows: one supports direct scientific manipulation and the other supports prediction, observation, hypothesis testing, feedback, comparison, and report export. Success means users can understand and reproduce a scientific state, identify whether inputs and results are valid, and complete the core workflows without relying on vision, color, or a pointer.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, and instructive. The interface should feel like a trustworthy classroom observatory: discoverable enough for a first lab, exact enough for technical inspection, and quiet enough to keep scientific evidence primary.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic glassmorphism dashboard, a decorative space-themed landing page, or a framework-demo card grid. Avoid gradient text, broad shadows, ornamental animation, tiny uppercase labels, ambiguous implementation terminology, excessive pill readouts, custom form controls, and scientific plots that are accessible only as pixels.

## Design Principles

1. Scientific correctness before convenience.
2. Simulation and Guided Labs are peer workflows.
3. Current state, pending changes, busy work, and failures are always explicit.
4. Charts remain primary evidence but never the only representation of information.
5. Guidance supports learners without obscuring expert controls.
6. Use density where comparison benefits and space where comprehension requires it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Core workflows target WCAG 2.2 AA. The product must support keyboard-only use, visible focus, 200% zoom and reflow, reduced motion, coarse pointers, low vision, color-independent plot encodings, and nonvisual equivalents for canvas output. Status, validation, lesson results, and recovery actions must be announced without live-region spam.
